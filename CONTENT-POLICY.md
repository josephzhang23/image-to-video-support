# Content Policy

**Effective date: July 18, 2026**

This Content Policy applies to the Photo to Video app by Joseph Works Limited. By using the Application, you agree to this policy in addition to the applicable terms of use.

## Prohibited Content

You may not use the Application to create, attempt to create, or share:

- Pornographic or sexually explicit content of any kind
- Sexualized depictions of real, identifiable people, including any form of non-consensual intimate imagery ("deepfake" content)
- Any content involving minors that is sexual, exploitative, or endangering
- Content that promotes violence, self-harm, or hatred against individuals or groups
- Content that harasses, threatens, or defames any person

This content is not permitted under any circumstances — regardless of whether the depicted person is real or AI-generated, and regardless of whether you have the depicted person's photo lawfully.

## How We Enforce This Policy

- **Automated moderation.** Every generation request is screened by automated content safety systems, enforced on our servers. Requests that violate this policy are blocked and are not generated.
- **Content removal.** Content found to violate this policy is removed within 24 hours of being identified or reported.
- **Account enforcement.** Accounts that violate this policy are blocked from the Application. We reserve the right to terminate access without refund for policy violations.
- **Legal cooperation.** We cooperate with law enforcement where required by law, including in cases involving non-consensual intimate imagery or content involving minors.

## Reporting Violations

If you encounter content that violates this policy, email **support@josephworks.app** with a description and, if possible, a screenshot. Reports are reviewed promptly, and reported content that violates this policy is removed within 24 hours.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Continued use of the Application after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

## Contact

For questions about this policy, contact **support@josephworks.app**.
